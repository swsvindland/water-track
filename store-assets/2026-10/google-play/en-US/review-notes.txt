Pendum Hydration is a paid-download Health & Fitness app with no sign-in, subscription or in-app purchases. No review account is needed; all core screens are accessible after installing the app. Health sync and notifications are optional.

1. On Today, choose a serving size and tap a favorite. Open the log/details to edit or delete that drink.
2. Open Edit favorites to configure visible drinks or add a custom favorite.
3. Open History and try day, week and month. Backdate a drink to populate another day.
4. In Settings, change the fluid goal, units, language and appearance. Enable reminders, allow notifications, and set a wake-up/bedtime schedule. Reminder actions log the default water size. Scheduled reminders are recalculated after app activity and may run out if the app is unopened for several days.
5. Enable optional health sync. BAC is disabled by default; enable it only to review the estimate and its safety explanation. BAC must never be used for driving or sobriety decisions, including at zero.

On Android, non-alcoholic fluid volume and caffeine (via Nutrition records) are exported to Health Connect; alcohol and BAC remain local.

There is no Wear OS app and no Apple Intelligence schedule setup on Android. A mirrored watch notification may offer the phone water-logging action.

Goal progress measures logged intake, not physiological hydration. BAC is a calculated estimate, not a measurement. The app is not a medical device. Background execution depends on the operating system.

Store screenshots use fictional demonstration records. Drink amounts and goals in the screenshots are examples.
