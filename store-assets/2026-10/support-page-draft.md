# Pendum Hydration support draft

For help with Pendum Hydration on iPhone or Android, email [monitored support email]. Include the app version, operating-system version, phone model and a short description of the issue. Please avoid sending private health records or photos unless necessary.

## Your local record

The app works without an account. It stores dated drinks, quantities, caffeine and alcohol content, favorites, goal, preferences, optional weight and BAC settings, reminder schedules and routine descriptions on your phone. There is no in-app backup or export. Deleting the app may remove these records; device backups depend on your settings. We cannot restore records from a developer server.

## Health sync

Enable health sync in Settings and grant the desired permissions. On iOS, non-alcoholic fluid volume, caffeine and standard alcoholic drinks are exported to Apple Health. Calculated event-time BAC estimates are exported when enabled. On Android, non-alcoholic fluid volume and caffeine (via Nutrition records) are exported to Health Connect; alcohol and BAC remain local. Both platforms may read the latest accessible weight for optional BAC estimates. Health weight is stored separately from manual weight. Editing or deleting a drink queues correction or removal of its linked exports on the next successful sync. Turning sync off stops future sync but leaves previously exported records in the health service. Background timing depends on your device; open the app to let pending work retry.

## Settings and corrections

Use Settings to choose units, language and appearance. Tap a logged drink to change its amount, caffeine, alcohol content or time, or delete it. Favorites and serving sizes can be customized. Coffee and tea count toward the goal; alcoholic drinks do not. Goal progress measures logged intake, not physiological hydration.

## Reminders and Apple Watch

Enable reminders and allow notifications, then set wake-up and bedtime. The plan is recalculated when you log or open the app; OS scheduling can delay reminders. Open the app periodically to refresh upcoming reminders. The Apple Watch companion requires the iPhone app; open both to refresh favorites and totals. There is no separate Wear OS app.

## BAC estimates

BAC is optional and off by default. It is not a measurement and cannot establish whether anyone is sober or safe to drive, including when it shows zero.
