Pendum Hydration stores dated drinks, quantities, caffeine and alcohol content, favorites, goal, preferences, optional weight and BAC settings, reminder schedules and routine descriptions in local app storage. You can use it without an account. The reviewed implementation has no advertising, analytics or developer-hosted record service. We do not receive your tracking records through the app.

## Reminders and companion devices

Reminders use local notifications, not a developer push service. Notification permission is optional. Turning off reminders stops future reminders. Reminder actions can log water. The Apple Watch companion exchanges goals, favorites, totals and queued drink entries directly with the paired iPhone using Apple's device connectivity services. Mirrored Android watch notification actions run on the phone; there is no separate Wear OS app.

## Routine descriptions

On compatible iPhones, optional Apple Intelligence schedule setup processes your routine description on the device. The app has no cloud-model fallback for that feature. You can edit the schedule manually.

## Optional health integration

Health sync is off until you enable it and grant permissions. On iOS, non-alcoholic fluid volume, caffeine and standard alcoholic drinks are exported to Apple Health. Calculated event-time BAC estimates are exported when enabled. On Android, non-alcoholic fluid volume and caffeine (via Nutrition records) are exported to Health Connect; alcohol and BAC remain local. Both platforms may read the latest accessible weight for optional BAC estimates. Health weight is stored separately from manual weight. Editing or deleting a drink queues correction or removal of its linked exports on the next successful sync.

Disable sync in Settings to stop future integration, and manage individual permissions in Apple Health or Health Connect. Disconnecting does not remove records already saved to the health service. Those services, operating-system backups and other apps you authorize follow their own privacy practices. Background processing is controlled by the operating system and may be delayed.

## Retention and deletion

Records remain locally until you delete them or app storage is removed. The app offers no in-app cloud backup or data export. Deleting the app may delete its local records; it does not remove prior health exports. Device backups may contain app data depending on your settings. Manage those backups with your operating system or backup provider. Delete records inside the app while health sync is enabled and let sync complete if you also want the app to remove linked app-origin health exports. Health records can also be managed in the health service.
